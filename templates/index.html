Questions & Answers

Explore common questions and answers about how WaterPulse uses Artificial Intelligence and environmental data to monitor and predict water resources.

WaterPulse is an AI-powered water intelligence platform designed to monitor, analyze, and forecast water resources. By combining environmental data—such as rainfall, temperature, humidity, reservoir levels, river flow, and water quality metrics—it helps users make informed decisions for sustainable water management.

The system uses trained Machine Learning models (such as Gradient Boosting, Random Forests, and time-series forecasters) trained on historical climate and hydrological data to predict reservoir capacities, rainfall runoff, and overall water availability.

WaterPulse evaluates critical quality metrics including pH levels, dissolved oxygen (DO), turbidity, conductivity, temperature, total dissolved solids (TDS), and contamination markers to ensure safe water utilization for communities, agriculture, and industries.

WaterPulse is built for municipal water supply boards, agricultural cooperatives, environmental protection agencies, researchers, and industries requiring reliable water forecasts and proactive risk mitigation tools.
